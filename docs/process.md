# How an email marketing agency runs (the process this OS is built on)

Email and SMS for online stores, built in the client's email platform. The database follows the work.
If your process is different, say so before the Interview questions, because a
different process changes the tables.

## 1. Onboarding (clients table, onboarding stage)
0 Paid → 10 Contract Sent → 20 Contract Signed → 30 Set-Up → 40 Call Booked → 50 Call Taken → 100 Onboarded.
Set-Up covers: the client's Slack channel, the shared folder, the onboarding form, access to the email platform,
the sending domain and the sign-up forms. Then a research document on the brand.

## 2. Flows (the Flows board)
Brief → brief amendments (bounce-back) → copy → design → design amendments (bounce-back) → design QA →
build in the email platform → live. One card is one flow. Its emails are a checklist inside the card.

## 3. Campaigns (the Campaigns board)
One card is one client's campaign calendar for one month: the whole batch moves together.
Calendar → amendments (bounce-back) → copy → design → design amendments (bounce-back) → design QA →
build in the email platform → scheduled → done.

## 4. Reporting (daily and weekly numbers)
Attributed revenue, open rate, click rate, placed-order rate and list size, per client, per day and per week.
After 2 weeks live, test one thing at a time: subject line, then offer, then design.

## 5. Everything else (the Tasks board)
Any one-off job for anyone. Stages: -1 Rejected (will not be done), 0 Backlog, 9 Blocked, 10 In Progress, 29 Amendments, 30 In Review, 100 Complete.
